**Gobble's Great Escape** — a top-down stealth-escape game in the browser. Thanksgiving is coming, and Gobble the turkey is on the menu. Sneak past the patrolling farmer, dodge his dog, slip through the barn, and survive the kitchen — the oven is lit and the carving knife is swinging — to reach the gate. Three stages (Farmyard, Barn, Kitchen), three lives, corn to grab, bushes to hide in, and a sprint that is fast but loud. Pure canvas, no libraries, no network: every sprite and sound is drawn and synthesised from code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD / arrows move, Shift sprint, H hide in a bush, click to walk, P pause, R restart, M mute. Reach the gate of each stage; escape all three to win.
